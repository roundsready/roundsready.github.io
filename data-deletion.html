Data deletion instructions

Applies to: Rounds Ready Uploader and Rounds Ready Poster · Last updated: 21 October 2026

These apps store no personal data about anyone. The only stored items are the access tokens of the channel owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

To remove the Facebook connection

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Select Rounds Ready Poster and choose Remove.

To remove the YouTube connection

Open Google Account → Third-party connections and remove Rounds Ready Uploader.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to moh.youniss7@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. Any stored token is deleted within 7 days and you receive a confirmation.
