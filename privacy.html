Privacy Policy

Applies to: Rounds Ready Uploader and Rounds Ready Poster · Last updated: 21 October 2026

What the apps are

Rounds Ready Uploader and Rounds Ready Poster are private tools used only by the owner of Rounds Ready | Clinical Academy. They publish the channel's own educational videos to its YouTube channel (@RoundsReadyRx) and to its Facebook Page. They are not offered to the public and have no other users.

YouTube (Google)

With the owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, Rounds Ready Uploader uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description, tags and privacy status;
  • set video thumbnails and upload caption files;
  • add videos to the channel's playlists;
  • post the lesson quiz as a comment from the channel under its own videos.

Facebook (Meta)

With the Page owner's permission, given through Facebook Login, Rounds Ready Poster uses the Meta Graph API to:

  • list the Pages the owner manages, so the owner can choose the Rounds Ready Page;
  • publish the Page's own lesson videos and Reels, with their text, thumbnail and caption files;
  • read the Page's name and follower count to show that the connection works.

What data is not collected

The apps do not read, collect or store viewer or follower data, messages, comments by other people, subscriber or follower lists, contacts, email addresses or any other personal information.

How data is stored

The only stored items are the access tokens (Google OAuth token and Facebook Page token), kept on the owner's private server so publishing can run. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Google API Services

Rounds Ready Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google: remove access at Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.
Facebook: remove the app at Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
In both cases the stored token is deleted from the server. See Data deletion instructions.
